**Arsenal 2-1 Leeds, Premier League, Emirates Stadium, 10 October 2026**

Arsenal came from behind to beat Leeds 2-1. James Justin put Leeds ahead, Riccardo Calafiori equalised, and substitute Bruno Guimaraes scored the winner. Arteta made three changes from the Brighton defeat: Ben White, Myles Lewis-Skelly and Noni Madueke started. Christos Tzolis was out; Kai Havertz kept his place despite a knock on international duty. Eberechi Eze and Viktor Gyokeres started on the bench.

- **How it unfolded**
  - Early, minute not stated: Noah Okafor's curling effort deflected inches wide off Bukayo Saka. Arsenal were caught offside four times in the opening 16 minutes.
  - 20': Madueke beat Jayden Bogle and crossed to the back post. Havertz's header did not beat James Trafford.
  - Later in the first half, minutes not stated: Anton Stach's goalbound effort was blocked by a teammate. Bogle forced David Raya into a save after getting away from Calafiori. Raya scrambled a looping header off the line. Okafor scored but was offside. Gabriel Gudmundsson's effort from the back post was parried by Raya. No half-time changes.
  - 55' (ten minutes into the second half): Gudmundsson's low cross reached Justin, who finished. The report says Gabriel tried to head clear and Madueke let the full-back run off him. Leeds 1-0. Before the goal, Madueke had squared for Martin Odegaard, whose shot lacked power and Trafford saved, and Ao Tanaka had threatened.
  - 62' (seven minutes later): Madueke's deep cross found White, who headed back across goal, and Calafiori volleyed in from close range after Havertz drew two defenders. Arsenal 1-1.
  - Straight after the equaliser: Jurrien Timber, Bruno Guimaraes and Gyokeres replaced White, Lewis-Skelly and Havertz.
  - 69' (seven minutes after the equaliser): Madueke found Odegaard, the ball went to Saka, and Declan Rice's backheel let Guimaraes finish into the bottom corner. Arsenal 2-1.
  - After that, minutes not stated: Saka shot over, and Odegaard missed from close range after Rice escaped his marker. Piero Hincapie and Mikel Merino came on late for Calafiori and Odegaard (exact minute not stated). Gyokeres missed a chance after Trafford and Tarik Muharemovic got in a mix-up. In the dying seconds Trafford saved Saka's effort after Gyokeres played him through.
- **Arteta's comments**
  - "We weren't pleased about what we did against Brighton, and today was an opportunity to prove that right. And the context took us to the probably most difficult one, to really prove that we are made of something that is special and unique. And we certainly reacted in a manner that we expected."
  - On going behind: "Yes, because when you play 12.30 at home and then you go one-nil down, it's a lot to do. You have to restore a lot of things and the energy is one of them. We had an incredible response immediately after that goal."
  - On the winner: "It was good. The players are unpredictable to hit different spaces. The timing, I think, the connection and the execution of it, it was a beauty."
