**Arsenal 2-2 Newcastle, Premier League, St James' Park, 21 November 2026**

Arsenal drew 2-2 at St James' Park. The point kept them six points clear at the top of the Premier League and ended a run of five league wins. Eberechi Eze scored in the 33rd minute, Harvey Barnes equalised in the 49th, Malick Thiaw put Newcastle ahead in the 62nd, and Noni Madueke levelled in the 78th. Mikel Arteta made one change from the win over Hull, with Eze in for Christos Tzolis. Bruno Guimaraes started on his first return since leaving in the summer. David Raya went off with a shoulder injury and Kepa Arrizabalaga replaced him. William Saliba was on the bench. Bukayo Saka and Martin Zubimendi were out injured, and Max Dowman was suspended. Chris Kavanagh refereed in front of 52,661.

- **How it unfolded**
  - Bruno Guimaraes took the kick-off to a hostile reception. 6': Eberechi Eze set up Noni Madueke, who cut inside Dan Burn and hit the post. Arseblog says Lukas Hornicek probably had the shot covered.
  - Martin Odegaard almost played Riccardo Calafiori through, and the pass was cut out.
  - 22': Jurrien Timber found Kai Havertz, whose snapshot went wide. 23': Amar Dedic was booked.
  - 28': Madueke took two Newcastle defenders with him and played Declan Rice on the overlap. Bruno Guimaraes could only prod the ball wide.
  - A Jacob Murphy shot cannoned off Harvey Barnes and dropped wide, with David Raya scrambling. Newcastle had little else before the opener.
  - 33': Havertz robbed Lewis Hall and played Eze in behind Dedic. Eze finished past Hornicek. Arsenal 1-0.
  - 43': Madueke took the ball off Hall, ran through and forced Hornicek into a low save. 45': Calafiori and Odegaard played him in again, and Hall got back to block the shot with Hornicek beaten.
  - 46': Matthias Jaissle sent on Tino Livramento, Jacob Ramsey and Anthony Elanga for Dedic, Sean Steur and Jacob Murphy.
  - David Raya went down after a blow to the head, diving at Joe Willock's feet.
  - 49': From a Calafiori throw-in, Odegaard miskicked under pressure from Willock. William Osula played Barnes through, and Barnes finished low past Raya. Newcastle 1-1.
  - 52': Willock shot wide. 56': Rice was booked for a foul on Hall. 58': Barnes was booked for a foul on Ezri Konsa. 60': Lewis Miley shot at Raya.
  - 62': Under pressure, Raya kicked the ball behind for a corner. Hall's delivery found Dan Burn unmarked, and his header back caused a scramble. The ball came off Willock and Malick Thiaw scored from close range. The goal stood after a VAR check. Newcastle 2-1.
  - 70': Raya went off with a shoulder injury, holding his left arm, and Kepa Arrizabalaga replaced him. Myles Lewis-Skelly replaced Guimaraes and Christos Tzolis replaced Eze.
  - Newcastle kept coming. Willock's deflected shot spun wide, Havertz headed a corner onto his own crossbar, and Kepa smothered the ball at Miley's feet after a flick from Osula.
  - 78': Odegaard released Madueke, who cut inside and scored with a low shot. Hornicek got two hands to the ball. Newcastle 2-2.
  - 81': Burn and Madueke were booked. 83': Yoane Wissa replaced Willock.
  - Thiaw flicked a corner wide of his own goal. Nine minutes were added.
  - 90+2': Sven Botman replaced Hall. 90+4': Viktor Gyokeres replaced Havertz and Mikel Merino replaced Odegaard.
  - 97': Ramsey shot over. 98': Gyokeres curled a shot over. 90+10': Gyokeres was booked.
- **Arteta's comments**
  - "My feeling is that we lost two points today. I think we were exceptional in the first half, at every level. Our intensity, the clarity that we had, how to attack the jumps of Newcastle every time they wanted to go man-to-man, the composure that we had, the way we used our relationship to break the press. And it should have been two, three or four-nil up, clear. But it wasn't. When it wasn't, then we are expected in the second half to again raise the level, because the opponent is going to have a reaction. And the opponent did have a reaction, and we were very below the level that is required to stretch the gap in a game like this, in this stadium. And then we gave them belief. And I say we gave them belief because we gave them a goal. We gave them a throw-in, where we just gave them the ball. We had no structure to control the opposite winger. We conceded a goal, and then it became a very chaotic game - every set-piece, we know that they are exceptional at it. And we conceded from a set-piece. And then you have to react. And then we went back to the exceptional team that we were in the first half, again, for another 25 minutes. And we scored a great goal. We created infinite openings to create and score the third goal, but we lacked some time, or precision to make that count."
  - On what changed after the break: "They were very similar. I don't think they were any different. They were jumping the same way, with the same duel, with the same adjustment that they were doing. They were much more efficient because they won the first duel and a lot of second balls. We gave some sloppy balls and created really bad circulation against these kinds of jumps. And we gave them belief. And belief is everything in football, especially when you play in this beautiful stadium atmosphere. And they scored the goal that we gave them. They scored another one. And then we had to react. But we certainly did that, and I really liked the reaction of the team. But that's why I said that, in my opinion, we lost two points today."
  - "I think, individually, we have some top performances for 70 minutes. But at this level, it's not enough. At this level, it requires 96 minutes, unless you score three or four goals. And then you can allow yourself, for 15 or 20 minutes, not to be at your best. And that's the next step that this team has to make."
  - On Bruno Guimaraes: "Very good. We spoke about that before the game. Because inevitably, emotionally, you have so much going on, especially in the way that he feels connected to this club, being the captain and everything that they did together. But I think he was very composed; he was very, very, very focused on what he had to do in the game. And I think for the time that he was on the pitch, he had a really good performance."
  - On Raya: "Well, unfortunately, he had to come off, which is never good news. He will get assessed now, and probably he will have an MRI tomorrow to see what it is exactly he has done. It was a knock. I don't know how it happened, and what the injury is at the moment, so we'll have to wait and see tomorrow."
