**Arsenal 2-0 Everton, Premier League, Emirates Stadium, 24 October 2026**

Arsenal beat Everton 2-0 in the rain. Ezri Konsa and Gabriel scored. Arteta made five changes from the Bayern draw: Ben White, Myles Lewis-Skelly, Mikel Merino, Eberechi Eze and Viktor Gyokeres came in. The report says Bukayo Saka was expected to miss the next 6-8 weeks with a quad injury. Piero Hincapie was fit enough for the bench after an ankle problem.

- **How it unfolded**
  - Arsenal settled into a back three, with Lewis-Skelly inverting and Merino pushed further forward. Eze got in behind before the opener.
  - 7': Viktor Gyokeres assisted Ezri Konsa, who scored from close range. It was Konsa's first Arsenal goal and Arsenal's first set-piece goal of the campaign. Arsenal 1-0.
  - Later in the first half: Tyrique George flashed an effort wide. Gabriel cleared a bouncing ball off the line after David Raya struggled with a corner. Eze curled wide. Madueke fired wide after Declan Rice found him. James Garner blocked a Merino effort. Madueke took a high ball on the turn and flashed a shot inches wide. Just before half-time White headed a backpass straight to Thierno Barry and Raya saved. Merino headed a Rice free kick over. The report says Arsenal had ten corners in the half. No half-time changes.
  - Early in the second half: Merlin Rohl's looping cross almost dropped into the top corner with Raya beaten.
  - Gabriel landed awkwardly after clearing a cross. The report says it looked like a bad knee or hamstring injury. He had treatment and continued. Arteta said Mosquera was ready to come on and that Gabriel seemed to be okay.
  - 68': Riccardo Calafiori, Bruno Guimaraes and Kai Havertz replaced Lewis-Skelly, Merino and Gyokeres. Gabriel headed in White's back-post cross. White was onside. Arsenal 2-0.
  - 80': Martin Zubimendi and Max Dowman replaced Rice and Eze. Kiernan Dewsbury-Hall headed inches wide from James Garner's cross.
  - Late: Zubimendi was penalised for a foul on Barry and went down with a bloody nose after an elbow from the striker. Dowman beat Garner with a piece of skill and was pulled back, but the foul was given against Dowman.
- **Arteta's comments**
  - "It's a big win for us. It was a tough week, the schedule that we had to go through, the amount of minutes and the games, were coming thick and fast. We knew that we had a very good opponent in front of us, but I think overall we have a really good performance, won the game and fully deserve to win it as well."
  - On the five changes: "Delighted. I think we were, for the most part, the better team and we dominated the game. Everton had their spells, like is usual as well. They have a lot of threat. But yeah, the fact we changed five players and still played at that level, it's impressive."
  - On the set-piece goal: "Yes, I hope now people are enjoying it, because they've been saying that we don't score from a set play! So we did."
  - On Gabriel's injury scare: "By the reaction of his teammates, it was a really worrying action. We know, Gabi, how tough and strong he is, and he seems to be okay."

  - Bench: Kepa, Mosquera, Timber, Calafiori, Hincapie, Zubimendi, Havertz, Guimaraes, Dowman.
