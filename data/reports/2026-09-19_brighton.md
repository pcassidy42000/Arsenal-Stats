**Arsenal 0-3 Brighton, Premier League, Amex Stadium, 19 September 2026**

Brighton ended Arsenal's unbeaten start, winning 3-0 at the Amex. Pascal Gross and Charalampos Kostoulas scored from range in the first half, and Chema Andres headed in from a corner. Arseblog called it a humbling afternoon. A win would have matched Arsenal's best start to a season in 123 years. Bruno Guimaraes and Jurrien Timber made their first Premier League starts of the season, the only changes from the win at Sunderland.

- **How it unfolded**
  - Early: David Raya passed straight into touch twice. Guimaraes gave the ball away and Kostoulas forced a save. Bukayo Saka and Guimaraes then got in behind, and Riccardo Calafiori's header from a Saka cross was blocked.
  - 31': Gross, given time on the edge of the box, curled a low shot inside Raya's near post. Brighton 1-0.
  - Later in the first half: Kostoulas missed a chance when left in space, and Yasin Ayari hit the side-netting. Martin Odegaard's shot was flicked goalwards by Saka on a counter. 45': Brighton went straight up the other end and Kostoulas shot between Ezri Konsa and Gabriel from 20 yards. Brighton 2-0.
  - Half-time: Arteta made no changes. Raya kicked the ball out of play inside 30 seconds of the restart.
  - 57': Kai Havertz lost the ball and conceded a corner. Chema Andres headed in off the upright from the delivery. Brighton 3-0.
  - 60': Arteta took off Guimaraes and Timber. Declan Rice moved to right-back, and Mikel Merino and Martin Zubimendi came on. Pascal Strujik then headed inches wide from another corner as Raya flapped.
  - 73': Viktor Gyokeres replaced Martin Odegaard, and Eberechi Eze replaced Christos Tzolis. Gyokeres's first touch was a handball. Calafiori had flicked a header over just before that change, and the report says it was greeted with jeers.
  - Late: Saka's shot was blocked. Havertz was through and shot straight at Verbruggen. 81': Max Dowman replaced Bukayo Saka. Rutter diverted an effort just over, and Raya twice denied O'Reilly. 90+7: Rice was booked.
- **Arteta's comments**
  - "Extremely disappointed. But first of all, I think we have to congratulate Brighton for the victory. I think they deserve it. I think they were the better team. And from our side, there were certain basics that you need to accomplish in football, especially in the Premier League, that we were very far from."
  - "A very disappointing result and performance. Full credit to Brighton in the manner they performed and went about the game. They were another gear from us in the basics of the game. They were always first in the first duel, in the second actions, second balls, and they started to play from there."
