**Arsenal 2-1 Slavia, Champions League, Fortuna Arena, 4 November 2026**

Arsenal won 2-1 at the Fortuna Arena. Noni Madueke and Viktor Gyokeres scored before half-time, and Wiktor Nowak replied in the 90th minute. Mikel Arteta made six changes from the win at Liverpool: Ben White, Piero Hincapie, Martin Zubimendi, Mikel Merino, Eberechi Eze and Gyokeres came in. William Saliba was back in the squad for the first time this season and stayed on the bench. Sky Sports said the win moved Arsenal up to third in the Champions League league phase.

- **How it unfolded**
  - 9': Gyokeres held the ball up and slipped Noni Madueke in behind. Madueke finished right-footed through Nazar Domchak's legs, with Oskar Kubiak close by. The goal stood after an offside check. Arsenal 1-0.
  - 16': Eberechi Eze set up Mikel Merino, and Domchak tipped his shot over from the edge of the box.
  - 20': David Moses was booked.
  - 24': Gyokeres headed Madueke's cross back across goal and Merino nodded over.
  - 26': Eze beat a defender with a dummy and Moses blocked the shot.
  - Slavia's only other first-half opening was a long throw.
  - 38': Gyokeres played a one-two with Merino, then curled in off the post at the far corner. Arsenal 2-0.
  - 42': Gabriel's header from a corner was cleared off the line.
  - 46': Ange N'Guessan and Mojmir Chytil replaced Danijel Sturm and Mubarak Suleiman. 47': Kubiak's shot from the edge was pushed away by David Raya.
  - 53': Ezri Konsa was booked for a challenge on Emmanuel Ayaosi.
  - 62': Cristhian Mosquera and Christos Tzolis replaced Konsa and Eze.
  - 68': Declan Rice was booked.
  - 73': Domchak saved Madueke's shot from an angle.
  - 75': From a corner, Gabriel headed the ball off the line and Raya pushed the follow-up around the post.
  - 78': Piero Hincapie's backheel left David Zima with a chance and he shot over. Jurrien Timber and Martin Odegaard replaced Ben White and Gyokeres.
  - 80': David Jurasek's cross found Mikulas Konecny, who could not direct the header.
  - 83': Stepan Chaloupek blocked Odegaard's shot. Kubiak then met a cross from Tomas Holes, and Raya got a hand to the header. It hit the post and then hit the goalkeeper.
  - 90': Jurasek crossed into the six-yard box and Wiktor Nowak scored from close range. Arsenal 2-1.
  - 90+3': Max Dowman replaced Madueke. 90+4': Stepan Chaloupek headed over. 90+6': Mikulas Konecny's shot was gathered by Raya, and Arsenal saw the game out.
- **Arteta's comments**
  - "First of all, you have to value the fact it's very difficult to win away from home. We've done it against a very tough opponent, we expected that toughness. Two very different halves - the first we played at a really high level in terms of intensity, tempo, quality and clarity in what we had to do, and what the game was demanding. We were very efficient - we should have scored three, four or five. We didn't make that gap, and at half-time, the message was clear. There was still a lot to do in the game - this was a team we were up against with huge spirit, and if you give them something, they will grab it. Right from the beginning of the half, we were extremely far from our standards, the basics, the concepts, and everything we discussed to avoid. It was far from what we expect from each other, but we found a way to hold onto the result and win the game."
  - "It's a huge lesson, and we'll learn from the second half. It's our third away game in quick succession, and we're the only team to have played three away games in the Champions League and just one at home - there are a lot of factors - but I don't like to use that, because in the first half we were at the level we wanted. I take full responsibility for the drop in the second half."
  - On Viktor Gyokeres: "I thought he was really good. It was a game we believed would be really good for him, for the demands the opponent puts in and the spaces he could use. He's done it really well, not just for the assist and the goal, but he was really connected to the team. It's really great news for him, because he really needed that performance."
