**Arsenal 1-0 Forest, Premier League, City Ground, 18 October 2026**

Arsenal edged Nottingham Forest 1-0. Substitute Noni Madueke scored the only goal, his shot deflecting off Jair Cunha. Arseblog said it was Arsenal's only shot on target. Arteta made five changes from the Lille win: Ben White, Riccardo Calafiori, Martin Odegaard, Kai Havertz and Bukayo Saka came in. Martin Zubimendi was on the bench after an ankle knock. Ilan Meslier was included instead of Kepa.

- **How it unfolded**
  - Forest's first sight of goal came after they had barely touched the ball for 20 minutes. Calafiori sold Gabriel short, Forest broke, and David Raya dealt with Dan Ndoye's near-post effort.
  - Later in the first half: Gabriel's backheel from a set-piece scramble was blocked by Ezri Konsa. Havertz headed over from a free kick, then fired into the side netting after Odegaard played him through. Just before half-time Liam Delap drew a foul from Declan Rice. Bruno Guimaraes blocked the free kick and took a whack to the head.
  - Half-time: Madueke replaced Eberechi Eze.
  - Early in the second half: both of Delap's efforts were deflected wide by Gabriel.
  - Arsenal still had not tested Matz Sels. 66': Jurrien Timber, Mikel Merino and Viktor Gyokeres replaced White, Guimaraes and Havertz.
  - After that change: Timber's cutback found Rice unmarked and he ballooned the shot over.
  - 80': Madueke drove inside and shot. The effort deflected off Jair Cunha and into the far corner. Arsenal 1-0.
  - After the goal: Delap's throw sent Arnaud Kalimuendo away. The report calls the throw illegal. Chris Wood was in front of goal and Rice blocked the effort. Murillo beat three defenders and shot inches over. Konsa then raced back to deny Delap.
- **Arteta's comments**
  - "We've done something that we didn't do in the last two seasons [at the City Ground], for sure, which is to win the game. We knew that the margins were going to be really small, unless you open up the game much earlier, which we had two big chances to do."
  - "They are a team that are extremely well coached, so well organised in the game. The longer the game goes on, they have more belief, you have to take more risks, and that's when the game can break. But we talked about it; we have to be so patient without losing the capacity to threaten."
  - The day after, on Konsa and Gabriel: "The win was not possible without them." On the late blocks: "Unbelievable, Ezri's and Declan's, I think Gabi had another one – that showed spirit and was a really good example of how much we want to win."
