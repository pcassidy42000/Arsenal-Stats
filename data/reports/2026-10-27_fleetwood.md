**Arsenal 1-1 Fleetwood, League Cup, Highbury Stadium, 27 October 2026**

Arsenal drew 1-1 away to League Two Fleetwood Town and lost the Carabao Cup fourth-round tie 5-3 on penalties. Aaron Loupalo-Bi scored for Fleetwood and Viktor Gyokeres equalised with his first goal of the season. Arteta made nine changes from the Everton win, with debuts for Illan Meslier and 18-year-old Ceadach O'Neill.

- **How it unfolded**
  - ~2': Referee Michael Salisbury stopped play because a drone was flying above the stadium. The stoppage lasted about eight minutes. Play resumed with Piero Hincapie heading over from close range.
  - Max Dowman was played through and scuffed his shot wide, with Viktor Gyokeres sliding in alongside him.
  - 21': Aaron Loupalo-Bi held off Marli Salmon and finished left-footed through Illan Meslier's legs. Harrison Neal assisted. Fleetwood 1-0.
  - Dowman picked out Kai Havertz, who headed wide. Another Dowman cross found Jurrien Timber, who headed wide.
  - 46': Ben White replaced Jurrien Timber, and Martin Odegaard replaced Marli Salmon.
  - 50': White switched the ball to Ceadach O'Neill, whose cross into the box reached Gyokeres. He finished from close range. Arsenal 1-1.
  - Odegaard set Dowman away and Jay Lynch saved the shot. Finley Potter then headed the ball away with Havertz waiting on the rebound. Havertz missed another chance after Odegaard played him through. Later Havertz delayed a cutback and a Fleetwood defender blocked it in front of Myles Lewis-Skelly.
  - 66': Eberechi Eze replaced Ceadach O'Neill.
  - 82': Ezri Konsa replaced Myles Lewis-Skelly, and Bruno Guimaraes replaced Martin Zubimendi.
  - 85': Max Dowman was sent off after reacting to a challenge from Harrison Neal. Arteta did not look pleased as Dowman left the pitch.
  - Stoppage time: Lynch saved Ben White's back-post effort from Eze's cross on the line, and he also saved an Odegaard shot. Havertz headed wide.
  - Penalties: Fleetwood went first. Ronan Coughlan, Harrison Neal, Will Davies and Andy Cannon scored, and Conor Haughey converted the winner. Martin Odegaard, Kai Havertz and Viktor Gyokeres scored for Arsenal. Lynch saved Bruno Guimaraes's kick. Fleetwood won 5-3.
- **Arteta's comments**
  - On Dowman's red card: "It is difficult to see on the cameras so I prefer to watch it back properly and then I can assess the situation."
  - Asked if Dowman can learn from it: "Yeah, we will learn from everything."
  - Asked what he said to Dowman as he walked off: "Something that we discussed already before the match."
