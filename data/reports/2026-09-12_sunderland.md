**Arsenal 2-0 Sunderland, Premier League, Stadium of Light, 12 September 2026**

Bruno Guimaraes scored his first Arsenal goal and Bukayo Saka converted a stoppage-time penalty as Arsenal won 2-0 at Sunderland. David Raya saved Enzo Le Fee's penalty. Arseblog called it a ninth successive Premier League win, the first time Arsenal had reached that figure since the Invincibles.

- **How it unfolded**
  - 13': Christos Tzolis found Declan Rice, whose diagonal picked out Saka. Saka's touch ran through to Robin Roefs.
  - 16': Dan Ballard headed Granit Xhaka's free kick wide at the back post.
  - Later in the first half, minutes not stated: Reinildo was booked for bringing down Saka. Kai Havertz fired Odegaard's cut-back over, then forced Roefs into a save. Calafiori's penalty appeal after a tangle with Brian Brobbey was not given. Arteta was booked for leaving his technical area. In first-half stoppage time Xhaka stopped a Saka header from reaching Havertz. Ben White ended the half holding his groin.
  - Half-time: Jurrien Timber replaced White, and Bruno Guimaraes replaced Myles Lewis-Skelly.
  - 53': from a Danso long throw, the referee gave Sunderland a penalty for Ezri Konsa's challenge on Ballard. VAR upheld it. Raya tipped Le Fee's penalty onto the post.
  - Approx. 56' (three minutes after the penalty): Rice found Guimaraes on the edge of the box and he finished into the top corner via the underside of the bar. Arsenal 1-0.
  - After that, minutes not stated: Ballard cleared a Havertz effort off the line. Guimaraes fired wide. Timber headed in from Odegaard's free kick and was flagged offside. Raya made a double save from Mukiele after Xhaka's cross. Gabriel and Brobbey were booked.
  - Eberechi Eze and Martin Zubimendi replaced Tzolis and Rice, and Mikel Merino replaced Odegaard. Eze miskicked over on the counter.
  - 90+6: Saka went down under pressure from Reinildo after holding him off, and scored the penalty. Roefs got a hand to it. Arsenal 2-0. Six minutes had been added.
- **Arteta's comments**
  - "I'm extremely pleased to win again. We had a really tough week: Chelsea, Napoli, winning there and now against a really tough opponent in a difficult place. We had really good periods but didn't score, and after that they can suffocate you, especially when they decided to be really physical and very direct, and we had to overcome a situation that, in my opinion, was absolutely unacceptable at this level. I'm very worried and very sad because this can never be a penalty ever."
  - On the penalty against Konsa: "It's a judo roll from the opponent. Ezri [Konsa] does nothing. Just watch it. We have a lot of cameras and VAR we have to take responsibility to protect the game. Today the game was unprotected and it could have cost us the game and the momentum which is a really, really serious thing."
