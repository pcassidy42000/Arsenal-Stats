**Arsenal 3-0 Lille, Champions League, Emirates Stadium, 13 October 2026**

Arsenal beat Lille 3-0 to make it two wins from two in the Champions League group phase. Jurrien Timber, Eberechi Eze and Bukayo Saka scored, all in the second half. Arteta made five changes from the Leeds win: Timber, Bruno Guimaraes, Mikel Merino, Eze and Viktor Gyokeres started. Max Dowman, Marli Salmon, Ceadach O'Neill and Maalik Hashi were on the bench.

- **How it unfolded**
  - First attack, minute not stated: Timber was played in and cut back for Guimaraes, who fired wide from close range. Timber then crossed for Merino, who headed straight at the goalkeeper. Ayase Ueda had the ball in the net and was ruled offside.
  - With half an hour gone, the report says Arsenal were disjointed. Heading towards the break, Myles Lewis-Skelly's low cross from Eze's pass went to the goalkeeper with Merino waiting. Guimaraes then played Lewis-Skelly through, and Eze's shot from the penalty spot was straight at Berke Ozer.
  - Just before half-time, minute not stated: Guimaraes's shot from distance was deflected by Alex Sandro and Ozer palmed it away, then pushed Gyokeres's follow-up out for a corner. No half-time changes.
  - Second half, minute not stated: Guimaraes appealed for a penalty after Alex Sandro's challenge. Neither the referee nor VAR gave it.
  - Around the hour: as Arteta prepared a triple change, Madueke played Timber in and he dinked the finish. Arsenal 1-0. Lille coach Davide Ancelotti said his side had stayed in the match for 60 minutes and then conceded three goals in five minutes. The match report does not give Timber's goal its own minute. It says Eze scored within three minutes and Saka four minutes after Eze.
  - Immediately after Timber's goal: Martin Odegaard, Saka and Kai Havertz replaced Merino, Madueke and Gyokeres. Odegaard won the ball, Guimaraes played Saka through, and Eze buried the first-time cross, taking a nick off a defender. Arsenal 2-0. Eze then ran through after Lewis-Skelly's touch and Ozer saved the dink.
  - Four minutes after Eze's goal: Odegaard slipped Havertz through, Havertz cut back, and Saka curled into the far corner. Arsenal 3-0.
  - Ben White and Dowman replaced Timber and Eze. Dowman's shots were blocked.
- **Arteta's comments**
  - "It was a joy to watch. The three goals were unbelievable: the connections, the timing, the quality they delivered to score. Three different players and other players participated in the goals, so overall I'm very, very happy. We had moments – in the first half, especially – when we got into very dangerous positions but our technical quality wasn't good enough to deliver moments of real threat and to sustain the attacks, so we allowed certain transitions."
  - On Timber's goal: "The difference on the goal of Jurrien, he ran from the blind side, the ball comes and boom. The pass is good, the timing is good and the execution from Jurrien is brilliant."
