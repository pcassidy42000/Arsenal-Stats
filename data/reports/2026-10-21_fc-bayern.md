**Arsenal 2-2 FC Bayern, Champions League, Allianz Arena, 21 October 2026**

Arsenal drew 2-2 with Bayern Munich. Eberechi Eze and Declan Rice scored for Arsenal. Alphonso Davies and Harry Kane scored for Bayern. Arteta made two changes from the Forest win: Jurrien Timber and Noni Madueke replaced Ben White and Eze, before Eze came on for the injured Bukayo Saka.

- **How it unfolded**
  - 8': Saka pulled up trying to make a pass and signalled to the bench. The report says he pointed towards his left quad. Eze replaced him.
  - Before the opener, minutes not stated: Riccardo Calafiori fired over from a corner. Bayern broke from Harry Kane's quick goal kick, Davies squared to Ismael Saibari, and Serge Gnabry's shot was saved by David Raya.
  - 25': Martin Odegaard played Eze in over the top and Eze finished low past Manuel Neuer. Eze had been given offside; VAR awarded the goal. Arsenal 1-0.
  - 35': the quotes round-up says Davies equalised 10 minutes after Eze's goal. Kane dropped deep and found Davies, who curled past Raya. Bayern 1-1. Before that, Bruno Guimaraes missed a chance from a corner.
  - Second half, minutes not stated: Neuer stopped Guimaraes's downward header on the line and the ball hit the crossbar. Raya saved from Dayot Upamecano. Eze drew another save from Neuer after going past Konrad Laimer.
  - Just past the hour: Laimer's cutback found Kane, whose shot hit Timber's arm. Kane scored the penalty after a stutter. Bayern 2-1. The quotes round-up says the penalty was just past the hour; the match report does not give a separate minute.
  - Approx. 65' (25 minutes to play): Myles Lewis-Skelly and Viktor Gyokeres replaced Guimaraes and Kai Havertz. Bayern brought on Luis Diaz and Lennart Karl for Saibari and Gnabry.
  - 82' (eight minutes remaining): Rice broke from midfield and finished a move involving Madueke and Gyokeres. Gyokeres held off Jonathan Tah and slid the ball through. Arsenal 2-2.
  - Late, minute not stated: Timber curled an effort from distance and Neuer saved. Nothing came of the corner in added time.
- **Arteta's comments**
  - "Wow, what a game, that's the level. That's one of the highest levels that we played. The level of the opposition, what the game required, and our level as well. Two teams that want to press each other constantly, two teams that want to attack, that want to play. I think it was a fascinating game."
  - "We'll take the draw, but it doesn't mean we're really happy about it because we prepared a team to win the game – you could see that from right at the beginning, with the mindset, the purpose the adventure team had to be very dominant and cause Bayern problems. We pressed them, attacked them, and we were the better team for 20, 25 minutes and scored the goal. Unfortunately we had the injury to Bukayo which was a big setback, but Ebs jumps in and the team remains in the same way."
