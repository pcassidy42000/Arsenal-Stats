**Arsenal 1-0 Napoli, Champions League, Diego Armando Maradona Stadium, 9 September 2026**

Arsenal began the Champions League group phase with a 1-0 win in Naples. Martin Odegaard scored the only goal. Arseblog said Arsenal were dominant and should have won more comfortably. Arteta's side made four changes from the Chelsea game: Piero Hincapie, Mikel Merino, Eberechi Eze and Viktor Gyokeres came in. Cristhian Mosquera was injured, and Jurrien Timber was missing despite travelling.

- **How it unfolded**
  - Early, minute not stated: Bukayo Saka's bicycle kick was straight at Alex Meret after Gyokeres's trivela cross. Kevin de Bruyne then forced David Raya into a save, and Matteo Politano tested Raya from distance.
  - 20': Saka's deep cross found Eze, whose header back across goal gave Merino a free header from about six yards. Meret tipped it over. From the corner, Ben White was through and dribbled at Meret.
  - Minutes before half-time, minute not stated: Odegaard put Saka through and Saka skied the chance.
  - Half-time: Napoli replaced the injured Meret with Vanja Milinkovic-Savic. No Arsenal change is listed.
  - Early in the second half, minute not stated: Hincapie blasted over from about two yards after Odegaard and White combined.
  - ~60': Arsenal had 76% possession, according to the report. Bruno Guimaraes and Christos Tzolis replaced Merino and Eze. Tzolis blasted over with his first touch.
  - Kai Havertz and Noni Madueke replaced Gyokeres and Saka.
  - 75': Odegaard's shot went in off the post after wall passes with White and Tzolis. Arsenal 1-0. The minute is from Arseblog's post-match piece. Martin Zubimendi replaced White, with Declan Rice moving to right-back.
  - Late, minutes not stated: Madueke was denied one on one, and Tzolis's close-range strike was blocked on the line. De Bruyne got free and crossed instead of shooting. Arteta's TNT interview also said Havertz had a goal ruled out for offside and that Arsenal had 26 efforts, eight on target.
- **Arteta's comments**
  - "I think we fully deserve three points. I don't know the amount of chances and situations we generated. But I think the performance probably doesn't reflect the results. I think it should have been much bigger in a really tough environment, [against a] really tough opponent. I think the team was superb."
  - On what he told the players while the game was still goalless: "To insist and generate more. That's what we have to do."
