**Arsenal 2-1 Chelsea, Premier League, Emirates Stadium, 6 September 2026**

Arsenal came from behind to beat Chelsea 2-1 and stay perfect in the Premier League. Morgan Rogers scored early for Chelsea; Kai Havertz and Martin Odegaard replied. Ezri Konsa made his full debut in place of the injured Cristhian Mosquera.

- **How it unfolded**
  - 2': Rogers volleyed past David Raya from a set piece. Chelsea 1-0.
  - Riccardo Calafiori bundled in from a set piece after Emi Martinez saved from Gabriel, but Ezri Konsa was ruled offside. Saka then forced another Martinez save, and Wesley Fofana blocked a Havertz effort. Raya saved from Cole Palmer and Reece James.
  - 25': Havertz cut in from the right and rolled the ball into the near post. Arsenal 1-1. The minute is from Arseblog's quotes round-up.
  - 45': Palmer was booked for tripping Havertz. Pedro Neto hit the post in first-half added time.
  - 50' (five minutes after the break): Tzolis drove inside and played the ball to Havertz, who left it for Odegaard to finish. Arsenal 2-1.
  - 60': Chelsea brought on Malo Gusto and Pep Chavarria. They tested Raya twice.
  - 67': Arteta responded with Piero Hincapie and Martin Zubimendi for Calafiori and Myles Lewis-Skelly.
  - After that: Saka's curling effort was saved by Martinez, and a corner ended with the ball in Martinez's hands.
  - 77': Mikel Merino replaced Odegaard. 78': Viktor Gyokeres replaced Havertz.
  - 90': Noni Madueke replaced Bukayo Saka. Deep into added time: Raya tipped Estevao's shot over the bar. The report's expected-goals line for the game was 2.27 to 0.36.
- **Arteta's comments**
  - "I was very disappointed with the way that we conceded the first goal, and we talked about it, but it's as well a credit to them and the finish from Morgan (Rogers). But the way that we reacted and the way that we played – it was a fascinating game. I think from start to finish, it was an absolute joy to watch our team play, compete, the composure, the personality, the threat that we generated, some huge individual performances."
  - On Odegaard: "First of all, he feels fit and confidence-wise, it's the highest I've seen him. He has a point to prove from last season as well, from missing so much football."
