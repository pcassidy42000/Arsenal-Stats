**Arsenal 3-0 Man City, Community Shield, Millennium Stadium, 16 August 2026**

Arsenal won the Community Shield for the 18th time, beating Manchester City 3-0 in Cardiff. Arseblog called it an imposing display, with Bruno Guimaraes and Christos Tzolis starting their first competitive games for the club.

- **How it unfolded**
  - 1' (25 seconds): Riccardo Calafiori finished after Myles Lewis-Skelly's reverse through ball. Arsenal 1-0.
  - 20': David Raya pushed away Foden's curling effort, and White was fouled by O'Reilly while clearing.
  - 22': Noni Madueke held off Doku and found Bruno Guimaraes on the overlap, but the pass into the box did not find a teammate.
  - 28': Tzolis headed Martin Odegaard's cross back across goal and Kai Havertz headed in. Arsenal 2-0.
  - Minute not stated: Calafiori went down in the box and was booked for simulation.
  - 40': Haaland's shot was straight at Raya, and White blocked Doku from close range.
  - Later in the first half, minute not stated: Calafiori volleyed over from White's cross, and Odegaard's shot after Donnarumma spilled Havertz's cross was saved off the line.
  - Half-time: Declan Rice replaced Bruno Guimaraes.
  - Early in the second half, minute not stated: Odegaard beat Gvardiol and slotted in after a pass from Tzolis. Arsenal 3-0.
  - 60': Piero Hincapie and Bukayo Saka replaced Calafiori and Madueke. Saka then shot into the side netting.
  - 74': Cherki's shot forced a save from Raya.
  - 75': Viktor Gyokeres replaced Havertz and Martin Zubimendi replaced Lewis-Skelly.
  - 80': Saka shot over from Hincapie's cutback.
  - 84': Odegaard was booked for a late challenge on Grealish, then Eberechi Eze replaced him.
- **Arteta's comments**
  - "A really, really good performance. It's so early in the season to play at the level that we've done it and to show the desire that we showed in every action, I was really impressed. I said to them I really enjoyed watching them play."
  - On Tzolis: "He's very, very smart, very accurate. And again today, in tight spaces, he made the right decision twice and he set up two goals."
