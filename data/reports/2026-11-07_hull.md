**Arsenal 1-0 Hull, Premier League, Emirates Stadium, 7 November 2026**

Arsenal beat Hull City 1-0 at Emirates Stadium and went six points clear at the top of the Premier League. Martin Odegaard scored in the 72nd minute, finishing the rebound after Konstantinos Tzolakis saved Noni Madueke's near-post shot. Mikel Arteta made six changes from the win at Slavia Prague and restored the side that won at Liverpool: Jurrien Timber, Riccardo Calafiori, Bruno Guimaraes, Odegaard, Christos Tzolis and Kai Havertz came in. Bukayo Saka was injured and Max Dowman was suspended. William Saliba, on the bench in Prague, was left out. It was a seventh Premier League clean sheet. Farai Hallam refereed in front of 60,285.

- **How it unfolded**
  - Hull pressed from the start. 2': John Egan headed a deep corner just wide. Semi Ajayi's looping header was gathered by David Raya, and Ezri Konsa met an inswinging cross but the ball flicked wide as Gabriel tried to get to it.
  - Raya got down to save a low drive from Elliot Stroud, and Riccardo Calafiori blocked Mohamed-Ali Cho's follow-up.
  - ~25': Arsenal were flat despite having the ball. Declan Rice's effort from distance went wide, and half an hour in Tzolakis had still not been tested.
  - Rice, from a laid-off free kick, and Tzolis, with a header, then drew comfortable saves. A move down the right between Kai Havertz and Timber ended with Tzolis volleying over. Bruno Guimaraes found Odegaard, who played Havertz through; Havertz could not get the ball under control and was caught coming back onto his left foot.
  - 34': Lewie Coyle was booked. Just before half-time, Nobel Mendy pulled Havertz in the box. Nothing was given in real time. Hallam was sent to the monitor, agreed there had been a foul, then treated Havertz as offside and gave Hull a free kick. Rice headed straight at Tzolakis, and the half ended 0-0.
  - Arsenal moved the ball quicker after the break. Guimaraes had a shot charged down, and Coyle cut out a diagonal pass from Rice. Tzolakis saved a low shot from Madueke and got behind a curler from Tzolis.
  - 48': Lucas Gourna-Douath was booked. 60': Joe Gelhardt replaced Cho. 61': Brooke Norton-Cuffy replaced Coyle and was booked.
  - 63': Mikel Merino replaced Rice and Viktor Gyokeres replaced Havertz. 69': Piero Hincapie replaced Calafiori, Eberechi Eze replaced Tzolis, and Lucas Herrington replaced Ajayi.
  - 72': Madueke cut inside and shot toward the near post. Tzolakis parried it, and Odegaard finished the rebound. Arsenal 1-0.
  - Gyokeres, Merino and Eze all tested Tzolakis, mostly with comfortable shots. 75': Stroud was booked and replaced by Ryan Giles, and Christos Mouzakitis replaced Tim Iroegbunam. 87': Mendy was booked. 89': Martin Zubimendi replaced Guimaraes.
  - Late on, Eze, Odegaard and Merino combined to put Madueke through. He tried to round Tzolakis instead of shooting and was crowded out, and the ball was cleared off the line. Hull's long balls and free kicks did not lead to a chance, and Arsenal saw the game out.
- **Arteta's comments**
  - "I'm very happy with the performance, with the victory as well. It's time to praise my players because what they've done, not only today, but after the first international break, is just remarkable: the amount of games that we played, the quality of the game, the results that we managed to get. So credit to everybody. Today was a day that probably should have been very different because we missed so many chances, situations that are completely open to score a goal, but we have to wait and be patient until the last part of the game to make the difference and win the game."
  - On having to dig deep: "Yeah, especially if you don't open the games up earlier, you know that that's going to be more difficult, and you go a bit against the clock, and you give some hope to the opponent. But we kept generating chances, arrivals into the final third, shots; the final pass was missing. When we had the final pass, the last shot was blocked. But we kept insisting; we talked at half-time - if that's the case, we're going all the way to the last minute, and at some point it will come."
  - On the tempo: "Sometimes more patience, as well, because in the first half there were moments that in two passes we went to attack, and then we were stretched. So it's about dominating the tempo; that's football. When to accelerate it, when to stop it, when to attract the opponent, when to use the space that they're leaving behind. And we need to master that. If you shoot, things can happen. Maybe it will be a deflection, maybe it will be a handball, maybe it will be a rebound. If you don't shoot, none of that happens."
  - On the penalty review: "Obviously, when they called the ref to watch the monitor, I thought it was a penalty. On the bench, they said it's a penalty. Then I heard that the decision was made because of the offside position. So we don't have clarity yet on that. But thankfully it didn't cost us again."
  - "Consistency and keep going. We talked about this that it will happen at some point to break them down. We had some big moments that in my opinion we should have scored some goals but we don't hit the target, we don't get the final pass. So many situations where if you don't do that you have to rely on generating so much volume and at some point relying that you will get them down and that's the way we won the game. It's something that is happening a lot, and a few games as well we have been 1-0 down very early and we still manage to do that. Getting results is very, very important and I think we need to praise our players for the way they go about every single game. We have done really well from the international break up to this point."
  - On Madueke: "He's a great example for the rest of the group because before the first international break he wasn't playing. And what he did is instead of going on holidays he went to Portugal and got somebody to train with him and got in better shape and condition and immediately he got back into the rhythm. You can see now he is full of confidence and trying things and getting past players and he's impacting games with goals and assists and we are very happy with that."
  - On Bruno Guimaraes: "He's much better, he had a massive impact, he's hitting serious levels now, but we can still take him to another level. That's my conviction, I told him that in the dressing room - he owned a lot of moments in the game, but there is still much more to come."
  - On Declan Rice, who was substituted: "He's fine."
  - "I think we have some great moments. The thing is that we need to put the differences that we are creating with the opponent into the scoreline. That's what we need to improve."
