**Arsenal 1-0 Liverpool, Premier League, Anfield, 1 November 2026**

Arsenal won 1-0 at Anfield, their first league win there since 2012. Kai Havertz scored in the 16th minute from Declan Rice's cross. Liverpool had 17 shots and did not put one on target. Sky Sports and NBC Sports both had Arsenal three points clear of Manchester City at the top.

- **How it unfolded**
  - The opening was scrappy. Arsenal had more of the ball, mostly looking for Christos Tzolis on the left. Declan Rice had a wild effort. Cody Gakpo dragged a shot wide, and Jeremy Jacquet blocked a Tzolis shot.
  - 16': Martin Odegaard intercepted a loose pass from Alisson Becker. Bruno Guimaraes found Noni Madueke, and Rice overlapped. His low cross reached Kai Havertz at the back post. Arsenal 1-0. NBC Sports called it Rice's fifth Premier League assist of the season.
  - Straight after the goal, Ezri Konsa's poor touch forced Rice to concede a corner, and Liverpool spent the rest of the half on the front foot. David Raya punched clear and got to the ball ahead of Alexander Isak. Guimaraes had an effort diverted by Virgil van Dijk, and Jurrien Timber blocked Madueke from close range.
  - Milos Kerkez and Alexis Mac Allister shot wide, and Isak missed after a scramble. Florian Wirtz hit the crossbar with a free kick after a foul given against Timber. Mac Allister then headed over from Kerkez's cross. 45+2': Bradley Barcola was booked for a foul on Timber.
  - After the break Liverpool's tempo dropped, but Ronald Araujo and Kerkez still put crosses in. Arsenal sat deep and went long through Raya toward Havertz, Tzolis and Timber. 55': Madueke was booked for a foul on Barcola, and Isak shot into the Kop. Dominik Szoboszlai replaced Trey Nyoni before the hour.
  - 62': Myles Lewis-Skelly replaced Bruno Guimaraes. Havertz released Tzolis, whose cutback toward Odegaard was cleared over. Calafiori got in behind and van Dijk cut out the cross before Havertz could meet it.
  - 67': Milos Kerkez was booked for a foul on Madueke, and Rio Ngumoha replaced Cody Gakpo. 72': Lewis-Skelly was booked for a foul on Alexander Isak. 73': Tzolis met a Madueke cross first time and Alisson saved low.
  - 77': Timber and Konsa stopped Araujo. Mac Allister fouled Odegaard and then slapped his head. Timber reacted, and Madueke put his head toward Mac Allister and survived a VAR check. 80': Timber and Mac Allister were booked.
  - 84': Piero Hincapie replaced Tzolis and Viktor Gyokeres replaced Havertz, and Calafiori was pushed forward to help contain Ngumoha. 88': Mikel Merino replaced Odegaard. Victor Munoz's header was gathered. Six added minutes began with Ngumoha's deflected shot, which earned a corner. 90+3': Raya was booked for time-wasting. 90+7': Virgil van Dijk was booked. Arsenal saw the game out.
- **Arteta's comments**
  - On the win: "For everyone attached to this football club, it has been 14 years since we won here, it has been a long time. After the Fleetwood defeat, we spoke about things happening for a reason, and that has given us another spring, hunger and belief to come here and do it. What the team has done today has been incredible. We generated six or seven big chances but the game becomes chaotic when you misplace a pass or a clearance. These guys are running at you and have an unbelievable capacity to generate vertical attacks. The way the team sustained that was incredible for me."
  - On Declan Rice's assist: "Not just that, first of all, the movement to recognise the open situation to make the overlap, the timing of him playing the two-vs-one in the right manner and arriving from the far side constantly in the right space is something we discussed. In the second half, we had two big chances, there were a lot of moments where the team had some incredible actions."
  - On Liverpool having no shot on target: "I didn't know about that statistic but that's incredible. It didn't feel like this to be fair on the pitch because there were very little margins sometimes when they had the opportunity to shoot and it's a team that shoots a lot. You have to be so good at blocking shots and preventing those spaces for them to get into there. Again, it's a commitment of every single player in every action consistently to deliver those kind of stats and to do it against this team, it's something remarkable I would say."
