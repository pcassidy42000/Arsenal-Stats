**Arsenal 1-0 Liverpool, Premier League, Anfield, 1 November 2026**

Arsenal won 1-0 at Anfield, their first league win there since 2012. Kai Havertz scored in the 16th minute from Declan Rice's cross. Liverpool had 17 shots and did not put one on target. Sky Sports and NBC Sports both had Arsenal three points clear of Manchester City at the top.

- **How it unfolded**
  - The opening was scrappy. Arsenal had more of the ball, mostly looking for Christos Tzolis on the left. Declan Rice had a wild effort. Cody Gakpo dragged a shot wide, and Jeremy Jacquet blocked a Tzolis shot.
  - 16': Martin Odegaard intercepted a loose pass from Alisson Becker. Bruno Guimaraes found Noni Madueke, and Rice overlapped. His low cross reached Kai Havertz at the back post. Arsenal 1-0. NBC Sports called it Rice's fifth Premier League assist of the season.
  - Straight after the goal, Ezri Konsa's poor touch forced Rice to concede a corner, and Liverpool spent the rest of the half on the front foot. David Raya punched clear and got to the ball ahead of Alexander Isak. Guimaraes had an effort diverted by Virgil van Dijk, and Jurrien Timber blocked Madueke from close range.
  - Milos Kerkez and Alexis Mac Allister shot wide, and Isak missed after a scramble. Florian Wirtz hit the crossbar with a free kick after a foul given against Timber. Mac Allister then headed over from Kerkez's cross. Bradley Barcola was booked in first-half stoppage time for a tackle on Timber.
  - After the break Liverpool's tempo dropped, but Ronald Araujo and Kerkez still put crosses in. Arsenal sat deep and went long through Raya toward Havertz, Tzolis and Timber. Madueke was booked for a foul on Barcola, and Isak shot into the Kop. Dominik Szoboszlai replaced Trey Nyoni before the hour.
  - Havertz released Tzolis, whose cutback toward Odegaard was cleared over. Calafiori got in behind and van Dijk cut out the cross before Havertz could meet it. Tzolis met a Madueke cross first time and Alisson saved low.
  - Timber and Konsa stopped Araujo. Mac Allister fouled Odegaard and then slapped his head. Timber reacted, and Madueke put his head toward Mac Allister and survived a VAR check. The Arseblog report says Timber and Mac Allister were booked.
  - Rio Ngumoha replaced Cody Gakpo in the 67th minute. Daniel Munoz's header was gathered. Six added minutes began with Ngumoha's deflected shot, which earned a corner. Raya was booked for time-wasting. Arsenal saw the game out.
- **Arteta's comments**
  - Asked afterwards about the game not being fluid: "Very fluid at times. We generated six or seven big chances but we couldn't break out. When you misplace a pass or clearance these guys are running at you. Makes it incredible to me."
