**Arsenal 1-0 Villa, Premier League, Villa Park, 31 August 2026**

Arsenal made it two Premier League wins from two with a hard-fought 1-0 at Villa Park. Bukayo Saka scored the only goal. The report says neither side had a shot on target in the first half.

- **How it unfolded**
  - Early, minute not stated: Zion Suzuki came for a Declan Rice corner and Gabriel headed wide of an empty goal. Emi Buendia then hit the crossbar from distance; the report says Christos Tzolis had been fouled in the build-up and Jarred Gillett gave nothing.
  - Before the half-hour, minute not stated: Tzolis was booked. He later stopped a Villa break that the report says could have drawn a second yellow, but the referee did not show one. Matty Cash's kick on him nearly went unpunished.
  - Half-time: the half ended without a shot on target for either side. Eberechi Eze replaced Tzolis.
  - 55': Arsenal were awarded a penalty when Ian Maatsen tripped Saka. VAR overturned it because the contact was outside the box. Martin Odegaard curled the free kick inches wide.
  - On the hour: Cristhian Mosquera found Eze, whose pass released Riccardo Calafiori, and Saka finished the low cross. Arsenal 1-0.
  - ~70': Bruno Guimaraes and Mikel Merino replaced Myles Lewis-Skelly and Odegaard.
  - After that, minute not stated: Guimaraes sent Kai Havertz through and Suzuki saved. Havertz was later ruled offside. Arsenal saw out eight minutes of added time. The report describes only these three substitutions.
- **Arteta's comments**
  - "I'm really happy, it's a massive win. It's a tough place to come. And then the three points, I think we deserved it. We had a great squad performance, I would say, because with eleven [players] it wasn't enough, the finishers came in and did a magnificent job to finish off the game and win it. I know how tough it is to win here."
  - "I think it was a squad victory today. Everybody who was involved in the game in any capacity made a difference for us."
