**Arsenal 3-0 Coventry, Premier League, Emirates Stadium, 21 August 2026**

Arsenal opened the 2026/27 Premier League season with a comfortable 3-0 win over newly promoted Coventry. Kai Havertz, Bukayo Saka and Martin Odegaard scored, and Arseblog said the champions were in complete control.

- **How it unfolded**
  - Early: Christos Tzolis shot wide, then Saka's snapshot was deflected wide. From the corner, Tzolis's effort was cleared off the line with Carl Rushworth beaten.
  - 15': Calafiori's pass picked out Havertz, who swept home. Arsenal 1-0. 23': Saka made it 2-0, finishing after Tzolis's low cross was palmed to him. Arteta said both goals came inside 23 minutes.
  - Still in the first half: Odegaard played Ben White through and the shot was blocked, and Declan Rice whipped an effort just wide.
  - 49': Odegaard bundled in White's cutback. Arsenal 3-0. The report says Arsenal had done the job in 48 minutes, and that Odegaard did not hit the shot cleanly.
  - After that: Tzolis had one shot deflected wide and another saved by Rushworth.
  - 68': Martin Zubimendi and Noni Madueke replaced Rice and Saka.
  - 76': Eberechi Eze and Mikel Merino replaced Tzolis and Odegaard.
  - 81': Piero Hincapie replaced Calafiori.
  - Late: Jack Rudoni's unmarked header was saved by David Raya. Merino's low effort was also saved.
- **Arteta's comments**
  - "There was a lot to like in that performance. Right from the beginning we were really dominant, really flowing with the ball, we connected with each other in a really impressive way."
  - "We scored two brilliant goals; we should have scored the third before half-time as well. After half-time, we scored the third one with Martin, a bit lucky, and then after that we had to manage the game."
  - On the break: "We need to go for the third goal; this is the Premier League where two-nil doesn't give you any confidence to close the game."
