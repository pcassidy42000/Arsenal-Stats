**Arsenal 1-1 Borussia Dortmund, Champions League, Emirates Stadium, 24 November 2026**

Arsenal drew 1-1 with Borussia Dortmund at Emirates Stadium. Viktor Gyokeres scored in the 10th minute and Maximilian Beier equalised in the 55th. It was the first time Arsenal have dropped points at home in the Champions League league phase, and they stayed unbeaten in it. Mikel Arteta made six changes from the draw at Newcastle: Kepa Arrizabalaga, Ben White, Piero Hincapie, Myles Lewis-Skelly, Christos Tzolis and Gyokeres came in. David Raya, Bukayo Saka and Martin Zubimendi were out injured, and William Saliba was on the bench again. Dortmund arrived as Bundesliga leaders, still unbeaten. The point left Arsenal fifth in the Champions League on 11 points, with Dortmund seventh on 10. Maurizio Mariani refereed in front of 60,704.

- **How it unfolded**
  - 2': Christos Tzolis volleyed over after Martin Odegaard and Noni Madueke worked the ball down the right. Arsenal.com says Madueke crossed for an unmarked Tzolis, who volleyed into the ground and over the bar.
  - Gyokeres tried a shot from range that Gregor Kobel gathered. The shot map times an on-target Gyokeres effort at 4'. Tzolis headed another Madueke cross back across goal and it was not converted. A Ben White cross went just ahead of Gabriel and Piero Hincapie.
  - 10': Madueke rolled the ball to Gyokeres about 25 yards out, and Gyokeres drove it over Kobel. Arseblog says Gabriel played Madueke in, and calls the strike a knuckleball. Arsenal.com calls it the club's 400th goal in the Champions League. Arsenal 1-0. Madueke is the assist.
  - 15': Tzolis worked into the box and Kobel parried. The rebound reached Madueke at the far post and the shot was blocked. The shot map has Tzolis on target and Madueke blocked, both at 15'.
  - Around the 20-minute mark, Fabio Silva shot over. That was Dortmund's first attempt toward Kepa. The shot map also has Felix Nmecha over at 24', and Arseblog says both shot over from decent positions.
  - Hincapie got into the area and chose a cutback to Gyokeres, which was intercepted. Declan Rice, after a tackle on Jobe Bellingham, tried a dipping shot that skimmed over. Odegaard then sent Rice through and Nico Schlotterbeck stuck out a leg to block him. The shot map has Rice efforts at 33' and 35'.
  - 36': Odegaard squared for Gyokeres, who shot over. Arseblog says Madueke had caught Schlotterbeck in possession, Odegaard was flagged offside in the build-up, and Joane Gadou had kept him on. Sky's key moments time the miss at 35'. The shot map also has a Gyokeres effort at 41'.
  - 44': In first-half injury time, Gadou aimed for the near post and Kepa turned it away for a corner. Neither side changed at half-time. Arsenal led 1-0.
  - Straight after the break, Daniel Svensson found Beier, who had stolen a yard on Gabriel and miskicked from close range. Arsenal.com says he was no more than six yards out. Madueke then cut inside and curled a shot just wide. The shot map has him at 49'.
  - 55': Gadou carried the ball from centre-back, past Tzolis, and played Beier through. Beier cut inside Gabriel and finished low into the bottom corner. Arsenal 1-1. Gadou is the assist.
  - 58': Kobel saved a Hincapie header from a narrow angle. Madueke could not find a red shirt with a cutback. 62': White blazed over after Gyokeres and Hincapie worked the ball.
  - 64': Konstantinos Karetsas and Serhou Guirassy replaced Beier and Fabio Silva. 65': Jurrien Timber, Bruno Guimaraes and Eberechi Eze replaced White, Lewis-Skelly and Tzolis. Arseblog places both sets of changes with 25 minutes to play.
  - 67': Ryerson, set up by Svensson, forced a save from Kepa, and Bellingham tapped in the rebound. The flag was up for offside. The shot map has Ryerson on target at 68'.
  - 69': Eze's shot was deflected wide by Gadou. Arsenal.com says it went over for a corner. 71': Waldemar Anton blocked Madueke, and Gyokeres glanced a header wide. TNT times that pair at 71'. The shot map has Madueke at 70' and 72', and a Gyokeres header at 71'.
  - 75': Odegaard crossed and Gyokeres headed wide from close range. Arseblog and TNT say Hincapie got into his eye-line. Sky and TNT both time the miss at 75'.
  - 79': Odegaard's low free-kick ended with the ball hitting the post. Arseblog says Guirassy bundled it onto his own post twice from about three yards, and Kobel then claimed it. TNT says Hincapie prodded the ball back and a defender hit the post.
  - 84': Eze played Rice through and Rice hit the near post with his left foot, with Kobel beaten. 87': Mikel Merino replaced Gyokeres. 89': Filippo Mane replaced Gadou.
  - Four minutes were added. Hincapie won a corner, Kobel missed a cross, and the ball would not fall in the scramble. Mariani blew for full time.
- **Arteta's comments**
  - "I think it was a brilliant performance against a really strong side. We are very disappointed that we didn't make the difference count with the amount of work, chances and quality that we showed throughout the game. That's the feeling in the dressing room, that we have to reward ourselves much more when we have that kind of performance. There were a lot of situations to be really pleased with - the quality, the connections, the understanding of what we had to do in the game, the dominance that we showed. And how much we insisted that we kept going and going until the last second, the last corner that we had another chance, and that's the way to go. Sometimes in football this can happen, but if we continue to do that, I think it will be a big difference between the teams."
  - On Gyokeres: "Unbelievable [goal]. He's got the technique and the quality to do that. Not only that, I think his overall performance was very, very positive. He was, again, really connected to the team. We used him a lot with the ball, running in behind, connecting, short, bouncing balls. He was a real presence today. I'm really happy with that."
  - On Raya: "We have nothing to add to that, unfortunately. We need to know first how bad it is and whether he has a chance or not in the next few weeks."
  - On Sunday's game against Manchester City: "100 per cent. Tomorrow we will train, then we'll have a day to rest and two days to prepare for a big match. We know the history with these matches and I'm sure what we're going to face at the Emirates."
