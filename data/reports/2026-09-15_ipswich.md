**Arsenal 4-2 Ipswich, League Cup, Portman Road, 15 September 2026**

Arsenal won a Carabao Cup third-round tie 4-2 at Ipswich and went into the fourth round. Max Dowman scored twice on his first start of the season. Noni Madueke and Mikel Merino also scored. Anis Mehmeti and substitute Chuba Akpom replied for Ipswich. Arteta made nine changes from the Sunderland game. Marli Salmon partnered Gabriel in defence, with Myles Lewis-Skelly and Martin Zubimendi at full-back.

- **How it unfolded**
  - 7': Dowman won the ball, exchanged passes with Eberechi Eze, sent Cedric Kipre to the ground and finished past Christian Walton. Arsenal 1-0.
  - 16': Bruno Guimaraes pounced on Walton's loose pass, Merino won the next challenge, and Madueke rifled the ball in. Arsenal 2-0.
  - Later in the first half, minutes not stated: Kepa punched clear, Mehmeti fired over, and Gabriel cleared John McAteer's header. Viktor Gyokeres's shot was blocked as half-time approached.
  - Half-time: Ezri Konsa replaced Gabriel.
  - Approx. 47' (less than two minutes after the restart): Madueke fed Zubimendi, who played Dowman through to slot past Walton. The report says Arsenal would have had a penalty if the ball had not gone in, because it struck a hand on the way through. Arsenal 3-0.
  - Soon afterwards, minute not stated: Madueke steered an effort wide with the outside of his foot.
  - Before the hour: Guimaraes played Merino in and he curled a finish in off the post. Arsenal 4-0.
  - After that, minute not stated: Jack Clarke's cutback found Mehmeti, who scored past Kepa. Ipswich 1-4.
  - Minute not stated, after Mehmeti's goal: Theo Julienne made his first-team debut in place of Guimaraes, at right-back, and Zubimendi moved into midfield.
  - Ipswich's best spell followed, minutes not stated: Kepa gathered at Akpom's feet and saved a deflected Florentino effort.
  - Minute not stated: Martin Odegaard and Christos Tzolis replaced Eze and Madueke. Tzolis's shot was blocked at the near post.
  - Near the end, minute not stated: Ife Ibrahim replaced Zubimendi. The report says Arsenal finished with four teenagers on the pitch.
  - Stoppage time, minute not stated: Akpom's effort went in with a deflection off Konsa. Ipswich 2-4.
- **Arteta's comments**
  - "I'm really pleased with a lot of things that we've done tonight – the way we attacked the game, how dominant, fluent and aggressive we were right from the beginning to try to influence the results. We scored two very early goals, which gives us a great platform for the game. And individually some big performances as well. Some debuts tonight, which is great to see as well."
  - On Dowman: "A joy to have him." On the attention around him: "If he does what he does on the pitch it's not easy, I understand. But what we have to do is look after him. He's 16 years old. You don't see that in this league."
  - On Salmon, with Arseblog noting he is 17 rather than 16: "Marli as well, 16 years old, to play at that level with the physicality they have to be able to handle that level, it's remarkable."
